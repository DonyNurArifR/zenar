# Code of conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful, assume good faith, keep discussion on the work, and do not harass anyone. Harassment, threats, or personal attacks are not welcome in issues, pull requests, discussions, or any other project space.

To report a concern, use GitHub's private reporting from the repository's **Security** tab, or contact the maintainer through GitHub. Reports are handled privately. Reviewers may remove comments, commits, or contributors who break these rules.
